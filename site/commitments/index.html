Your data

Built for messages you'd rather nobody read

Local processing

Runs on a machine we control in the US, or on yours. No connectors into your systems: you send exports, we never hold a login.

AI off by default

The analysis is rule-based. An optional AI review runs only with your written approval, on a zero-retention API, never used for training.

Redacted and pseudonymised

Emails, phones, card and bank numbers, IBANs, SSNs and street addresses become placeholders. People appear as P1, P2… by default.

Deleted, with a receipt

Within 30 days of delivery, or 7 days after you ask. The receipt lists each file, its size and fingerprint.

Not for scoring people

Findings are prompts to check, not verdicts on anyone. The contract rules out using them for performance reviews.

Your people know first

We start only after you confirm the employee-notice checklist: lawful basis, notice, works council where needed, excluded channels.

Questions

Before you order

What happens after I pay?

Within one working day you get the statement of work, the DPA and the intake checklist at the email you used at checkout. Nothing is analysed until the checklist comes back confirmed.

What if it isn't a fit?

Full refund before we start. If you can't send compliant exports within 30 days, we refund all but USD 100. If we can't complete the analysis, full refund. Incomplete work is deleted either way.

Which tools do you read?

Email (Gmail Takeout, Outlook via mbox, maildir), Slack workspace exports, Jira, Zendesk and Linear CSV exports, and Zoom, Otter, Fireflies, Granola and Gong transcripts. Any mix, in one report.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. You send exports; we never ask for a login, an API key or an integration.

Is this a fraud investigation?

No. Payment-change and approval findings are things to check. We don't authenticate payment instructions or decide whether anything was fraud.

We're in the EU.

Tell us before you order. We'll attach the EU standard contractual clauses to the DPA and confirm where processing happens.
