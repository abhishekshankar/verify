10. Method & limits (honest)

Precision (share of flagged items a human agreed with). On this data, if measured; else our own labelled sets. Synthetic sets are small and written by us: a floor check, not a promise about your data.

SourcePrecisionSample
email (Enron, real)90%18/20 checked by hand
Slack (synthetic)87%13/15, 30 messages
tickets, Jira (synthetic)100%7/7, 26 messages
meetings, Zoom VTT (synthetic)100%9/9, 20 turns
risk flags (synthetic)88%7/8, 18 messages
  • Detection is rule-based. First-person promises ("I'll send", "we will review") in the writer's own text; quoted and forwarded history ignored. Meetings add decisions ("we decided", "let's go with") and named action items. Same-message actions ("attached", "below") and social offers dropped.
  • "No evidence" is not "not done". Evidence = a later linked reply from the owner with done words, a recipient acknowledgement, a ✅ reaction, or a resolved ticket. Phone calls, unexported tools and other threads are invisible.
  • Flags are prompts to check, not findings of fraud or misconduct. Payment-change flags use wording, sender history, lookalike domains, urgency and secrecy cues.
  • Messages link by reply headers, Slack threads, ticket IDs, the same meeting, quoting the promise's first 8 words, or the same non-empty email subject.
  • Sources: email IDs show as mail: + the first 8 hex of SHA-1(Message-ID); the full ID is origin in found.json. Slack, ticket and meeting IDs show as is.
  • One person = one label across sources: merged by shared email, full name, or a people list. Copies of one message are merged into the earliest (cited) with a copy count.
  • Names inside quoted text and subjects are not pseudonymised; only emails and phone numbers are redacted there.
